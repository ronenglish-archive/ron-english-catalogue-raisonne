About the Catalogue

Project History and Editorial Responsibility

In 2018, at the request of Ron English, his sister Susan English and her wife, Ximena Florez Castrillon, began organizing and documenting the artist's extensive personal and professional archives with the goal of creating a comprehensive catalogue raisonné of his work.

Working collaboratively, they share responsibility for archival research, artwork identification, classification, bibliographic documentation, digital image restoration, editorial decisions, and the development and maintenance of the online catalogue.

The compilers have direct access to original artworks, sketchbooks, studio records, photographs, negatives, correspondence, exhibition records, digital files, and previously unpublished archival materials entrusted to them by the artist. Ron actively assists with the identification, dating, and contextualization of his works.

Development of the website began in 2025. The initial phase of the catalogue encompasses approximately 18,000 individually documented artworks, including paintings, drawings, murals, billboards, NFTs, and comic book covers.

Future phases will incorporate sculptures, designer toys, installations, and other areas of the artist's practice, alongside an expanding collection of bibliographic, exhibition, and photographic documentation.

Research and Cataloguing Methodology

Primary archival materials are cross-referenced with documentation from galleries, collectors, publishers, exhibition records, and other external sources.

Titles are established through existing documentation or consultation with the artist. Where no established title is available, a descriptive title may be assigned by the compilers. Dates are recorded when known, approximated when sufficient supporting evidence exists, or left unspecified. Discrepancies between sources are noted where relevant.

Independently executed versions of a composition and separate preparatory studies are treated as distinct works. Multiple drawings on a single physical sheet may be catalogued individually or collectively, depending on their relationship to one another. All drawings on the same physical sheet share a Work ID.

Individual NFTs are catalogued separately. Identical copies, photographic reproductions, digitized versions of existing artworks, and derivative graphic adaptations are not counted as additional distinct works. Where relevant, these materials are incorporated into the supporting documentation of the corresponding artwork.

Only works whose authorship has been established to the satisfaction of the compilers are included. Questions of attribution are resolved through direct consultation with Ron English whenever necessary.

For information regarding catalogue use, citation, authentication limitations, corrections, and copyright, please consult the Terms of Use, Copyright & Research Notice.
